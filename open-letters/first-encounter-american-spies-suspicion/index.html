In late 2018 or early 2019, but most likely at the end of 2018, my wife met two individuals that I will only identify publicly as [Subject #2 - a U.S. intelligence operative in China] and [Subject #3 - a U.S. intelligence operative in China, wife of Subject #2]. Upon returning home from this initial encounter, she immediately shared her suspicions with me, explicitly wondering whether they might be associated with U.S. intelligence. My wife, being a Chinese citizen, was meeting Christian missionaries for the first time in her life, which understandably raised her suspicions. However, having grown up in a Catholic family and having previously encountered missionaries, I personally found their presence and activities perfectly normal.
