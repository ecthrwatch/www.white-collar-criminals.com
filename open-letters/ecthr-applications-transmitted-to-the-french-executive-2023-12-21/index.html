The applications filed with the European Court of Human Rights are transmitted to the President, the Prime Minister, the Foreign Minister and the Ambassador

2023-12-21 1 min read

Placeholder. This entry records the event and its source; the full text and the exhibits are still to be published.

The day after filing them, Vincent B. Le Corre transmits the applications submitted to the European Court of Human Rights to the highest levels of the French executive: the President of the Republic, Prime Minister Élisabeth Borne, Minister for Europe and Foreign Affairs Catherine Colonna, Ambassador to China Bertrand Lortholary, Consul General Guillaume Roy, and the ministry’s private office. The applications themselves are attached to the message.

This event matters for what follows: it establishes that, a week before the psychiatrist’s recorded assessment reached the French diplomatic post in Beijing, the substance of the Strasbourg proceedings had already been placed before the President, the Prime Minister and the Foreign Minister.

Nature of the document: original — the first time this content was sent.

Original language of the message: French.
